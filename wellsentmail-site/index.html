is this you?

Built for B2B teams with a list of dream accounts.

If one new client is worth $5,000 or more to you, the cost of a package is a rounding error. If someone on your team spends the week chasing meetings with accounts that never reply, those are the accounts I mail.

Usually a fit

  • Managed IT and cybersecurity providers selling multi-year contracts to businesses in their region
  • Staffing and recruiting firms trying to reach hiring managers who ignore them
  • B2B software and IT services companies with a sales team and a list of dream accounts
  • Agencies and consultancies that win on relationships

Not a fit

  • Anyone selling to consumers
  • Anything under a few thousand dollars a deal
  • Prospects who work remote-only or out of a coworking space. A package has to land on a real desk, and I won’t ship to one that isn’t there.

here’s the whole mechanism

How a campaign runs

01

We build the list together

You tell me who you want: the industry, the company size, the person. I pull the contacts, verify the emails, and check every mailing address twice before anything ships. Already have a list? Even better, send it over.

02

I build and ship the package

Something they’ll actually keep, with their company on it. Inside is a handwritten card in your rep’s name and a QR code to a page with the prospect’s name on it. Every card and email goes to you for approval first.

03

It lands, and your rep calls

Tracking confirms delivery and you get a heads-up. A few days later, your rep makes the easiest call of the week: “Hey, did the package show up?”

04

The emails run on their own

An email sequence goes out in your rep’s name, starting the day after delivery. If your team doesn’t call, the emails still do the work. If they do, the emails back them up.

worth answering upfront

Questions people ask early

What happens after I book a call?

We talk through who you sell to and pick the package that fits. Then I send a proposal with exact pricing for your list. Nothing ships until you’ve approved every card and email and paid upfront.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the package and the size of your list, so there’s no flat number on this page. I’ll walk you through exact pricing on the call and follow up with a proposal. If you want a feel for it first, the three package ideas come with a cost on each.

Who makes the packages?

I do. I source the item, personalize it, write the card, pack it, and ship it from Austin. Engraving in bulk is the one thing I send out.

Who makes the phone calls?

Your team does. When the package lands, your rep calls and asks if it showed up. It’s the easiest call they’ll make all week. I don’t sell calling, and if your team doesn’t call, the emails still do the work.

How fast can we start?

14–28 days from kickoff to the first package on a desk, depending on the item and the size of your list.

Is there a minimum order size?

Campaigns start at 25 prospects.

How does payment work?

100% of the project cost is due upfront at kickoff. There’s no deposit-and-balance split to track.

What happens if a package arrives damaged?

Tell me and I’ll reship it at no cost. I don’t carry pre-paid insurance on shipments, so flag it quickly and I’ll move fast.

How did you get my email?

If you got a cold email from me, I pulled your contact from a B2B database and you matched the kind of company this works for. Every email has a line at the bottom to make it stop, and I mean it.

What results should I expect?

I won’t quote you a number I haven’t earned yet. What I can tell you is that a package gets opened when an email often doesn’t, and that the “did it show up?” call gets answered. When I have a campaign’s real numbers to share, they’ll be on this page.

Do you handle email compliance?

Yes, every sequence follows standard CAN-SPAM opt-out practices.
