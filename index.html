worth answering upfront

Questions we get asked early

What happens after I book a call?

We’ll talk through your list and your goals, land on which campaign fits (Cover, Custom, or Holiday Gifts), then send a proposal with exact pricing for your volume. Nothing ships until you approve the scripts and pay upfront.

Who actually makes the phone calls?

We don’t sell calling or SDR services. If your team likes to call, they can use the package as a warm opener: “did you get what we sent?” But plenty of value comes from the mail and email sequence alone; calling isn’t required to see results.

What happens if a package arrives damaged?

Tell us and we’ll reship it at no cost. We don’t carry pre-paid insurance on shipments. Flag it quickly and we’ll move fast.

How fast can we start?

Fourteen days from kickoff to your first package delivered, across every volume tier: the Cover Campaign, Custom, or Holiday Gifts.

How is pricing set for Custom and Holiday Gifts?

You tell us your budget per prospect or per customer. That budget is what you pay. We go find an item that fits inside it. There’s no fixed menu to pick from.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your volume and which campaign you pick, so there’s no flat number to publish here. We’ll walk through exact pricing on the call and follow up with a proposal.

How does payment work?

100% of the project cost is due upfront at kickoff. There’s no deposit-and-balance split to track.

Is there a minimum order size?

The Cover Campaign starts at 25 prospects. Custom and Holiday Gifts scale to whatever list size you’re working with.

Do you handle email compliance?

Yes, every sequence follows standard CAN-SPAM opt-out practices.
