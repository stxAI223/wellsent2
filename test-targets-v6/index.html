Three real companies Lone Star EHS Partners could go after, and what we’d send each one

You replied to my email, so instead of a pitch deck I picked three companies you could go after. For each one: why they fit, the package, the handwritten card in your rep’s name, and the message your rep sends once it lands.

Target 01

AUMOVIO (New Braunfels plant)

Why they fit

AUMOVIO, formerly Continental's automotive group, opened its New Braunfels plant in 2022. It employs nearly 500 people building radar sensors, and in October 2025 it announced a $110 million, 65,000 sq ft expansion. More space, more automation and more new hires is when an EHS team needs extra hands.

The package

LED headlamp with their name laser-etched on the band

A bright USB-C rechargeable headlamp for early floor walks, with the EHS manager's name etched on the strap so it stays theirs.

Your rep’s follow-up after it lands

Hi [Name], just checking the headlamp made it to the plant. With the expansion adding a lot of new space and new hires, I figured your team has its hands full. Worth 15 minutes next week to see if we can take some of the audit and training load off?

Target 02

Ranch Hand (Lippert), Seguin

Why they fit

In November 2025 Seguin approved a deal for Ranch Hand, a division of Lippert, to build a 400,000+ sq ft plant on I-10. It's planned to add about 320 jobs and a new E-coat line with zirconium pre-treatment, and it consolidates work from the Shiner and Beeville sites. A new coating line and a big hiring ramp both bring permitting and safety-training work.

The package

Insulated thermos engraved with their name

A stainless thermos that keeps coffee hot through a full shift, with the recipient's name engraved on the side.

Your rep’s follow-up after it lands

Hi [Name], hope the thermos made it in one piece. Standing up a new E-coat line while bringing on a few hundred people is a lot. Open to a quick call about permitting and safety training support for the Seguin build-out?

Target 03

ECOR Global, Elmendorf

Why they fit

ECOR is building its first U.S. plant in Elmendorf, in Bexar County. It's a 300,000 sq ft facility making recyclable panels from agricultural waste fiber, with about 160 workers planned. A first U.S. plant means setting up EHS programs and permits from scratch.

The package

Leather work gloves with their initials debossed

Good leather work gloves with the recipient's initials debossed on the cuff, for site walks during construction and startup.

Your rep’s follow-up after it lands

Hi [Name], did the gloves make it to you? Opening a first U.S. plant means building the EHS program from scratch. Worth 15 minutes to talk through permits and training before startup?
