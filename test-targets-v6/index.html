Target 01

Toyota Motor Manufacturing Texas

Why they fit

Their San Antonio truck plant runs around the clock with thousands of team members and a big on-site safety team. EHS managers there are judged on incident rates and audit readiness, which is exactly what you help with.

The package

Rugged rechargeable LED headlamp

A bright, USB-C rechargeable headlamp for early walk-throughs and dark corners of the line. Something an EHS manager actually reaches for on shift.

Caterpillar's Seguin facility builds large engines and has a dedicated environmental, health and safety team that answers to strict corporate standards. Outside help on compliance and training is an easy conversation to start.

The package

Keeps coffee hot through a full shift and survives being dropped on concrete. Used every single morning.

Good leather gloves with the recipient's initials debossed on the cuff. Personal, useful on site walks, and they stay on the desk.
